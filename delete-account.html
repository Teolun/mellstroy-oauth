Delete Mellstroy account

Account and associated data deletion

Delete from the app

Open Mellstroy → Settings → Info → Delete account. Follow the confirmation or re-authentication steps shown in the app.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, send an account deletion request to the developer. Include enough information to identify the Mellstroy account, such as the account email, phone number, or username.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

The deletion process is intended to remove the Firebase account and associated app data handled by Mellstroy, including profile data, chat data, and watch-reward data where available.

Retention

Some information may be retained only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance, as described in the Privacy Policy.
