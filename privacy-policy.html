Privacy Policy

Mellstroy Android application · Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mellstroy Android application accesses, collects, uses, stores and shares information. The app may connect to third-party services including Google/Firebase, OneSignal and Kick. Those services may process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, the app may process identifiers and profile information such as Firebase user ID, email address, display name, username, surname, phone number when available, profile biography, profile photo or avatar URL, account status and authentication-provider information. Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database and Firebase Storage may be used to provide account and profile functionality.

3. Messages and user-generated content

If you use the in-app chat, the app may store messages, replies, sender identifiers, display names, avatar URLs, timestamps, read state, reports, blocked-user records and moderation-related information. If you choose to sign in to Kick and send a Kick chat message, the message and related account information are sent to Kick as part of the action you requested.

4. Notifications and device identifiers

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for stream and chat notifications. Firebase may process a push registration token and related device/app information required to deliver notifications. The app also contains OneSignal integration and may process a OneSignal device or subscription identifier for notification and profile-related functionality.

5. Watch activity and rewards

When watch-reward functionality is enabled and you are signed in, the app may send authenticated watch-session activity to the application's backend. This can include a session identifier, sequence number, app version, verified watch duration and reward-account state. Firebase Authentication and Firebase App Check / Play Integrity tokens are used to authenticate and protect these requests. These requests are intended to use HTTPS.

6. Camera, QR/barcode scanning and local media

Camera access is used only for features that require the camera, such as QR or barcode scanning. Scanning is designed to be processed on the device unless a feature clearly tells you that data will be sent elsewhere. Downloaded videos, exported clips and GIF files may be stored on your device. Local media remains on the device until you delete it, remove the app, or explicitly share it using Android system sharing.

7. Authentication with Google and Kick

Google Sign-In / Credential Manager may be used to authenticate a Firebase account. Kick OAuth may be used when you choose to connect your Kick account. Kick access and refresh tokens are used to call Kick services on your behalf for features you request, such as chat. Tokens are not intended to be sold or used for unrelated advertising.

8. Third-party service providers

The app may rely on Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Storage, Cloud Messaging and App Check / Play Integrity), Google Sign-In / Credential Manager, OneSignal, Kick services and the application's backend infrastructure. These providers may process information necessary to provide authentication, storage, messaging, notifications, security and stream-related features.

9. How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts and profiles, synchronize app data, provide chats and replies, deliver notifications, maintain moderation and blocking features, provide stream and playback features, maintain watch rewards, prevent abuse, protect backend services and troubleshoot service failures. The app does not sell personal information.

10. Data retention

Account, profile and server-backed app data may be retained while your account is active and for as long as necessary to provide the relevant feature. Security, anti-abuse or moderation records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary. Local downloads, clips and GIFs remain on your device until you remove them. OAuth credentials are retained only as needed for the connected service, until logout, revocation or expiration.

11. Account and data deletion

Users can start account deletion from Settings → Info → Delete account. The deletion flow removes the Firebase account and associated app data handled by the application, including profile, chat and watch-reward data where available. An external account-deletion web resource is also provided for users who cannot access the app. Where specific information must be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal reasons, retention is limited to the necessary purpose and period.

12. Security

The app uses platform security features and service authentication mechanisms including Firebase Authentication, App Check / Play Integrity and secure token storage where implemented. Network requests containing account or reward data are intended to use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

13. Children

This app is not designed for children and should not be presented as a child-directed service. Live streams and chat may contain mature or user-generated content. Age and content-rating settings in Google Play should reflect the actual released functionality.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data practices, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date shown below will be updated when material changes are made.
